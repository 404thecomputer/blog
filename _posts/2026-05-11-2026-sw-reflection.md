In utilizing web development skills to serve the greater community, knowing how to clearly communicate with the client to best understand what is needed. The client knows best what is needed, and it is my job to help carry out their goals through the website I create. It is my task to build the website around the goals, so clear communication with the client to understand the goals is key. Whenever our team met with our client, we made sure to ask clear clarifying questions to help build the image of the project such as what they seek to accomplish through the project and how different types of users should be able to interact with the finished product. The answers to these questions helped shape the way we built our projects

The client tasked our team with creating a website that displays funding opportunities to the public looking for ways to donate to the school's various projects, such as renovations, new equipment, and programs like Odyssey. It also is meant to help campus staff/faculty submit projects looking to be funded. I think that this could enact helpful change in the community if it was easier to donate to the college. Donations can help expedite projects and ultimately supports the college community by allowing such opportunities to be fulfilled. 

Long before taking on this project, I made several donations to my local animal shelter, such as sponsoring kennels and donating to construction projects. I even spearheaded a project to build a "puppy playground" at the animal shelter to allow dogs to run and play safely outside of their kennels. I took charge to try and earn funding for my project, so my beliefs in the effectiveness of allocating donations through methods of ease of access like the website were confirmed through working on this project. I hope that my contributions to this website can enact positive community change in the future if it gets enacted into future use.
